Four stages. Twelve weeks. One decision that changes everything.

The same framework I walk every client through, applied to your career, identity, time and future direction.

R

Recognise

Where you really are, not the version you tell people at school pickup. The honest one.

I

Integrate

What motherhood has built in you — and what your next career must provide that it didn't before.

S

Shift

The beliefs, patterns and invisible workloads keeping you stuck. Most women find this is where the real change happens.
